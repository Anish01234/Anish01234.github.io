Welcome to Kawaii Doodle ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our Android application, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using Kawaii Doodle, you explicitly agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

For information on how Google collects and processes data when you use our application, please visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Content Standards

Kawaii Doodle is a platform for sharing original hand-drawn artwork with friends and the global community. All user-generated content — including Doodles posted to the Community Feed, text comments, and custom profile pictures — must comply with our content standards. The following categories of content are strictly prohibited:

  • Sexual or explicit content: Nudity, sexually gratifying imagery, sexual fetishes, or content intended to cause sexual arousal.
  • Violence or graphic content: Gruesome, graphic, or gory imagery; depictions of real-world violence, torture, or execution.
  • Hate speech or discrimination: Content that incites hatred against, promotes discrimination of, or disparages individuals or groups on the basis of race, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, nationality, sexual orientation, gender identity, or other protected characteristics.
  • Harassment or bullying: Content that harasses, intimidates, or targets specific individuals for abuse.
  • Dangerous content: Content promoting or depicting the use of illegal drugs, weapons, explosives, or other dangerous items.
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM): Any content that sexually exploits, depicts, or endangers minors. See Section 11 for our full CSAM policy.
  • Spam or misleading content: Repetitive, off-topic, or deliberately misleading submissions.

We provide in-app reporting mechanisms for Community Feed Doodles and Comments. Users may report content using any of the following categories: Sexual or explicit content, Violence or graphic content, Hate speech or discrimination, Harassment or bullying, Drugs, weapons, or dangerous content, Spam or not a drawing, Underage or child safety concern, and Other. Reported content is reviewed by our moderation team, and violating content is removed. Accounts that repeatedly violate these standards are permanently banned.

In-app safety controls: Kawaii Doodle provides controls for user safety and moderation. Users may report a Community Doodle from the Community tab or the Doodle detail menu, report a comment from the comments sheet, block another user from that user's profile, delete their own comments, and delete their own Community Doodles from their History screen. Reported Doodles and comments are reviewed through an admin-only moderation panel where moderators can remove violating Doodles, remove comments, or dismiss reports after review.

Automated content filtering: All text comments submitted through the application are automatically screened against a prohibited-content keyword system before being posted. Comments matching prohibited categories are blocked in real time and are not published. This automated filtering system is implemented in compliance with Google's recommendations for managing user-generated content on ad-monetized platforms.

This content framework is maintained to comply with the Google Publisher Restrictions, which identify restricted content categories that affect advertising eligibility. Kawaii Doodle does not intentionally host content in any restricted category and actively moderates its Community Feed to prevent such content from appearing.

1. Who Can Use This App

Kawaii Doodle is intended for users who are 13 years of age or older. This application is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this app. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13 without verification of parental consent, we will delete that information promptly.

In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), all ad requests sent from this application are tagged as not child-directed. We do not use interest-based advertising targeting based on past activity by users known to be under the age of 13, and we do not serve such ads on screens directed at children. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us with personal information may contact us at kawaiiidcontact@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you interact with Kawaii Doodle:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and profile photo obtained via Google Sign-In or uploaded directly by you via the secure system picker.
  • Profile Data: Your chosen username and Kawaii ID.
  • User-Generated Content: Drawings ("Doodles") you create, transmit privately, or publish to the public Community Feed, as well as any text comments you post.
  • Social Data: Your friends list, user connections, and public interactions such as likes, reports, and blocks inside the app.
  • Device Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens used to deliver push notifications to your signed-in devices.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Your Android Advertising ID (AAID), used by Google AdMob to serve and measure advertisements. This identifier may be reset or deleted at any time via your device settings.
  • Device and Network Information: As a consequence of ad serving by Google AdMob, standard device and network data may be collected, including your device's IP address, device type, operating system version, and app identifiers. This collection is performed by Google AdMob's SDK and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Application Preferences: Settings such as dark mode, wallpaper preferences, and notification choices stored locally on your device.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or microphone data, and we do not require broad camera or storage permissions. To allow you to upload a custom profile picture, we utilize the secure Android system Photo Picker. This means our application only receives access to the single specific image you manually select, and we never have access to your device's full photo gallery.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • To create, manage, and authenticate your account.
  • To facilitate the transmission of Doodles with connected friends.
  • To display your public Doodles, profile, and text comments on the global Community Feed.
  • To deliver push notifications regarding new Doodles and relevant account activity.
  • To display and maintain your Doodle history and friend activity logs.
  • To provide, manage, and process Kawaii Premium subscriptions.
  • To display optional rewarded advertisements via Google AdMob, allowing free users to earn bonus doodle sends.
  • To enforce our safety policies and review reports of inappropriate user behavior.
  • To respond to customer support inquiries and requests.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties under any circumstances. We do not pass any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, or account ID, to Google AdMob or any advertising partner for targeting or identification purposes.

We do not use your data, or permit any third party to use it through our application, to target advertising based on sensitive personal attributes, including but not limited to health or medical information, financial status, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, sexual orientation, political affiliation, or trade union membership.

5. Advertising

Kawaii Doodle displays optional rewarded advertisements powered by Google AdMob. These ads are entirely voluntary. Free users may choose to watch a rewarded ad to earn a bonus doodle send.

Data collected by ad serving: As a consequence of displaying ads, Google AdMob's SDK may collect and process the following types of data from your device:

  • Android Advertising ID (AAID): A resettable device identifier used for ad targeting and measurement.
  • IP Address: Used for geographic ad targeting and fraud prevention.
  • Device identifiers and technical data: Including device type, operating system version, app version, and mobile network information, used to serve and measure ads.
  • SDK-level interaction signals: Equivalent to web beacons, used by AdMob to measure ad impressions and engagement.

All of the above data collection is performed solely by Google AdMob's SDK and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive, store, or have access to this data.

Personalization and consent: Upon first launching the app, users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland are presented with a consent form powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), in compliance with the EU User Consent Policy and the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). This form allows you to choose whether personalized advertising is permitted. Ads are only loaded and displayed after your consent preference has been recorded. For users outside regulated regions, non-personalized ad requests may be made in accordance with applicable standards.

Interest-based advertising (AdChoices): When personalized ads are enabled, Google may use your Advertising ID and other signals to show you interest-based advertisements. Personalized ads served by Google are marked with the AdChoices icon, which links to information about how the ad was targeted. This is consistent with applicable internet advertising industry guidelines, including the Self-Regulatory Principles for Online Behavioral Advertising of the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) and IAB Europe's EU Framework for Online Behavioral Advertising.

No sensitive category targeting: We do not permit targeting of ads through our application based on sensitive personal attributes, including health, financial status, race or ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, political views, or trade union membership.

How to opt out or change your ad preferences:

  • In-App (EEA/UK/CH users): Navigate to Profile → Ad Privacy Settings to revisit and update your ad personalization choices at any time.
  • Device-Level Opt-Out: On Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads and enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android 12 and below, or reset/delete your Advertising ID on Android 13+.
  • Google Ad Settings: You may also manage your Google ad personalization preferences at adssettings.google.com.

Kawaii Doodle does not pass any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, or account ID, to Google AdMob for advertising purposes. All data collection by Google AdMob is governed by the Google Advertising Policies and the Google Privacy Policy. For more information on how Google collects and processes data when you use our app, please visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6. Push Notifications and Background Processing

Kawaii Doodle requests permission to send push notifications to inform you when a connection sends a Doodle or interacts with your content. We also request the battery optimization exemption on Android to ensure Firebase Cloud Messaging can reliably deliver notifications even when the application is running in the background. This permission is used solely to maintain notification delivery infrastructure. We do not execute any other unrelated background activity. You may revoke notification permissions at any time via your device's operating system settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for the duration that your account remains active. Should you choose to delete your account, all associated data, including your profile details, uploaded profile pictures, Doodle history, public comments, and connections list, will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days of the deletion request.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You maintain the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You may view your profile information and Doodle history directly within the application.
  • Data Deletion: Navigate to Profile → Delete Account to permanently remove all your data from our systems. You may also delete individual comments and Community Doodles directly within the app.
  • Subscription Management: Navigate to Profile → Manage Subscription to modify or cancel Kawaii Premium at any time.
  • Ad Personalization: Navigate to Profile → Ad Privacy Settings, where available, to update your advertising consent choices at any time.
  • Advertising ID: You may reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time via Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device. Deleting your Advertising ID will prevent personalized ad serving.
  • Notification Opt-Out: You may disable push notifications entirely via your Android system settings.
  • Direct Inquiries: For any other data-related requests, please contact us at kawaiiidcontact@gmail.com.

Access to user data is strictly limited to authorized services necessary for app functionality. While no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, we undertake commercially reasonable steps to protect your personal information.

11. Child Safety & Reporting

Kawaii Doodle strictly complies with all relevant child safety laws and has published standards prohibiting Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward any form of abuse.

In-App Feedback Mechanism: If you encounter Doodles, text comments, or user behavior that violates our policies, we provide a direct in-app mechanism for user feedback. You may report public Doodles, comments, and other user-generated content through the relevant in-app report controls. You may also block users from their profile page.

Addressing CSAM: We are committed to taking appropriate action to address Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any user found distributing, promoting, or interacting with CSAM will be immediately and permanently banned. We actively report all instances of CSAM to the relevant regional and national authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

Child Safety Point of Contact: Our designated point of contact is ready and able to speak about our app's CSAM prevention practices and compliance. For any child safety concerns, please reach out to us at kawaiiidcontact@gmail.com.
