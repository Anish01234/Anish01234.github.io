Welcome to Kawaii Doodle ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our Android application, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using Kawaii Doodle, you explicitly agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Who Can Use This App

Kawaii Doodle is intended for users who are 13 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this app. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13 without verification of parental consent, we will delete that information promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us with personal information may contact us at kawaiiidcontact@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you interact with Kawaii Doodle:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and profile photo obtained via Google Sign-In.
  • Profile Data: Your chosen username and Kawaii ID.
  • User-Generated Content: Drawings ("Doodles") you create and transmit within the application.
  • Social Data: Your friends list and user connections inside the app.
  • Device Tokens: A Firebase Cloud Messaging token utilized to deliver push notifications.
  • Application Preferences: Settings such as dark mode, wallpaper preferences, and notification choices stored locally on your device.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, microphone, or camera data.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • To create, manage, and authenticate your account.
  • To facilitate the transmission and receipt of Doodles with connected friends.
  • To deliver push notifications regarding new Doodles and relevant account activity.
  • To display and maintain your Doodle history and friend activity logs.
  • To provide, manage, and process Kawaii Premium subscriptions.
  • To respond to customer support inquiries and requests.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Services

Kawaii Doodle employs the following third-party service providers, each governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In: Utilized for secure authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Utilized to deliver push notifications. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Supabase: Serves as our backend database and API. Your data is securely stored on Supabase servers. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: Utilized to securely process Kawaii Premium subscriptions. We do not store or process your direct payment details. Google Payments Privacy Policy

5. Push Notifications and Background Processing

Kawaii Doodle requests permission to send push notifications to inform you when a connection sends a Doodle. We also request the battery optimization exemption on Android to ensure Firebase Cloud Messaging can reliably deliver notifications even when the application is running in the background. This permission is used solely to maintain notification delivery infrastructure—we do not execute any other background activity. You may revoke notification permissions at any time via your device's operating system settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for the duration that your account remains active. Should you choose to delete your account, all associated data—including your profile, Doodle history, and connections list—will be permanently expunged from our servers within 30 days of the deletion request.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You maintain the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You may view your profile information and Doodle history directly within the application.
  • Data Deletion: Navigate to Profile → Delete Account to permanently remove all your data from our systems.
  • Subscription Management: Navigate to Profile → Manage Subscription to modify or cancel Kawaii Premium at any time.
  • Notification Opt-Out: You may disable push notifications entirely via your Android system settings.
  • Direct Inquiries: For any other data-related requests, please contact us at kawaiiidcontact@gmail.com.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security practices, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), for all data transmitted between the application and our servers. Access to user data is strictly limited to authorized services necessary for app functionality. While no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, we undertake commercially reasonable steps to protect your personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy periodically. Upon doing so, we will amend the "Effective Date" at the top of this document. Your continued use of the application following such changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. For material changes, we will provide notification via the application or email.

10. Reporting Inappropriate Content

If you encounter Doodles or user behavior that violates our policies, you may report the incident directly within the app. Navigate to the History tab, select the three-dot menu adjacent to the respective Doodle, and select "Report" to notify our moderation team. We review all submitted reports and execute appropriate disciplinary action, which may include content removal or permanent account suspension.

If you have any questions, concerns, or formal data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at:
