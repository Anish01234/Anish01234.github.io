🎨 Privacy Policy

Kawaii Doodle  ·  Last updated: March 31, 2025

Welcome to Kawaii Doodle ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our Android app, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using Kawaii Doodle, you agree to this policy.

1. Who Can Use This App

Kawaii Doodle is intended for users who are 13 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this app. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us with personal information may contact us at kawaiiidcontact@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Kawaii Doodle:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and profile photo obtained via Google Sign-In
  • Profile data — your chosen username and Kawaii ID
  • Doodles — drawings you create and send within the app
  • Friends list — the users you connect with inside the app
  • Device token — a Firebase Cloud Messaging token used to send you push notifications
  • App preferences — settings such as dark mode, wallpaper preferences, and notification choices stored locally on your device

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, microphone, or camera data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To let you send and receive doodles with friends
  • To deliver push notifications about new doodles and activity
  • To display your doodle history and friend activity
  • To provide and manage Kawaii Premium subscriptions
  • To respond to support requests you send us

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Ever.

4. Third-Party Services

Kawaii Doodle uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In — used for authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — used to deliver push notifications. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — used as our backend database and API. Your data is stored on Supabase servers. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — used to process Kawaii Premium subscriptions. We do not store your payment details. Google Payments Privacy Policy

5. Push Notifications and Background Processing

Kawaii Doodle requests permission to send push notifications so you know when a friend sends you a doodle. We also request the battery optimization exemption on Android to ensure Firebase Cloud Messaging can reliably deliver notifications even when the app is in the background. This permission is used solely to maintain notification delivery — we do not run any other background activity. You can revoke notification permission at any time in your device settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all of your data — including your profile, doodles, and friends list — is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access — you can view your profile and doodle history directly in the app
  • Delete your account — go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data
  • Manage subscription — go to Profile → Manage Subscription to cancel Kawaii Premium at any time
  • Notification opt-out — disable notifications at any time in your Android settings
  • Contact us — for any other data requests, email kawaiiidcontact@gmail.com

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data transmitted between the app and our servers. Access to user data is restricted to authorized services only. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For significant changes, we will notify you via the app or email.

10. Reporting Inappropriate Content

If you encounter doodles or user behavior that violates our policies, you can report them directly in the app. Tap the three-dot menu on any doodle and select "Report" to notify our moderation team. We review all reports and take appropriate action, including removing content or suspending accounts.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
kawaiiidcontact@gmail.com
