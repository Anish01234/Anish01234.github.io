🗑️ How to Delete Your Account

Kawaii Doodle — Complete Data Deletion Guide

In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

The easiest way to delete your account is directly in the Kawaii Doodle app:

  1. Open Kawaii Doodle
  2. Tap your Profile icon (bottom right)
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account
  4. Read the first warning dialog and tap Continue
  5. Read the final confirmation and tap Delete Everything
  6. Your account and all data will be permanently deleted
⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your doodles, friends list, and account data will be destroyed immediately.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your Kawaii Doodle account, the following data is permanently removed:

✓ Deleted immediately:

  • Your profile (name, username, Kawaii ID, avatar)
  • All doodles you sent to friends
  • All doodles in your inbox from friends
  • Your complete friends list
  • Draft doodles (unsent)
  • Your auth account and login credentials

What Gets Kept

The following is not deleted when you delete your account:

  • Doodles you sent to others will remain in their inboxes (they own copies)
  • Your friends will still have records of past doodles you sent them

Data Retention Timeline

Account deletion happens immediately in-app. Any residual data in backup systems is permanently purged within 30 days.

Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot delete your account through the app, you can request deletion by emailing:

kawaiiidcontact@gmail.com

Include your Kawaii ID or the email address associated with your account. We will process deletion requests within 7 days.
