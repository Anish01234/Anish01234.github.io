Account Deletion

Data Removal and Retention Policy

In-App Deletion (Primary Method)

Users may initiate a complete account and data deletion request directly through the Kawaii Doodle mobile application. This is the fastest and most secure method of removal.

  1. Launch the Kawaii Doodle application on your device.
  2. Navigate to your Profile (bottom right navigation).
  3. Select Delete Account at the bottom of the profile menu.
  4. Review the impact of deletion and select Continue.
  5. Confirm your intent by selecting Delete Everything.
Irrevocable Action Deletion is permanent. Once the process is finalized, Kawaii Doodle cannot restore your account, recover lost drawings, or reinstate connection lists.

Data Impact Analysis

The following table outlines how your data is handled upon the successful execution of a deletion request.

Purged Items

  • User Profile (Name, ID, Avatar)
  • Inbound Doodle Inbox
  • Active Connections (Friends List)
  • Local and Cloud Auth Credentials
  • Unsent Drafts and Temporary Files

Residual Records

  • Doodles sent to other users*
  • Recipient Inbox History
  • Aggregated, anonymous analytics

*Note: Per our privacy policy, when a Doodle is sent to a recipient, a copy is generated for their inbox. These copies remain accessible to the recipient even after the sender's account is purged.

Retention and Purge Timeline

Upon confirmation, your primary account records are restricted and removed from active production databases immediately. Residual data stored in encrypted off-site backups is systematically purged within a 30-day window to ensure complete data sanitization.

Manual Deletion Requests

If you are unable to access the mobile application, you may submit a formal deletion request via email. To verify your identity, please contact us from the email address associated with your account:

Submit Request kawaiiidcontact@gmail.com
Please include your Kawaii ID if available. Processing time: 7 business days.
