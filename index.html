What it is

A small drawing can say more than a long message.

Kawaii Doodle helps you send quick hand-drawn doodles to someone close. Instead of being just another notification, the doodle becomes a little personal moment on their lock screen.

It is made for the tiny things: a heart, a silly face, a reminder, a “thinking of you,” or a cute sketch that only your friend would understand.

Why it feels special

It is not perfect art. It is personal attention.

Fast enough for everyday use, cute enough to feel warm, and personal enough to stand out from normal chat apps.
